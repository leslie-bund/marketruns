# Item vocabularies grow per market, not from a central catalog

The shared vocabulary that structured intents match against is grown per market from the shop items sellers actually register; the platform curates normalization and synonyms, not a canon of items. We rejected a centrally governed global catalog because it cannot keep pace with what individual Nigerian markets actually sell, and freeform text because it breaks the matching filter that bounds intent fan-out.

## Consequences

- A new market bootstraps its vocabulary from its first sellers' shop items.
- Synonym curation is an ongoing operational cost, budgeted per market.
